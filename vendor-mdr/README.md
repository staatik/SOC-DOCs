# Vendor & MDR Management

## Contents

- **SLAs** — per vendor, response-time and coverage commitments.
- **Escalation contacts** — named contacts and escalation paths per vendor, kept current (this is exactly the kind of content that goes stale fastest — set a short `review_cadence_days`).
- **Review cadence** — schedule for periodic vendor performance reviews.
- **Renewal triggers** — contract renewal dates and the lead time needed to re-evaluate before auto-renewal.
- **Coverage boundaries** — what the MDR is and isn't responsible for, cross-referenced against `domains/incident-response/` so there's no ambiguity mid-incident about who's driving.
